Lecture 6

Flows, symmetries and the symplectic form

Lecture 5 made the observables an algebra. A time-independent observable changes along a motion at the rate of its bracket with HHH. This lecture asks what flow an observable generates, and what that flow preserves. Every observable fff generates a flow, as HHH generates the motion. Momentum generates translations, and angular momentum generates rotations. The Jacobi identity ensures that the flows preserve the bracket. Brackets of observables determine Lie brackets of their vector fields. Noether’s theorem now works in both directions: a quantity is conserved exactly when HHH is invariant under its flow. The bracket also determines a bilinear form ω\sympω, a sum of oriented areas. The flows preserve this form, so their Jacobian matrices are symplectic. Quadratic Hamiltonians give the Lie algebra sp(2n)\splie(2n)sp(2n). These results prepare the course’s second answer in Lecture 7.
